## Overview

shadcnpreset is a free, open-source companion to shadcn/ui presets. A preset bundles a style, colours, radius, fonts and icons into one short code, and shadcnpreset lets you see them on real UI before you commit: compare presets side by side, browse the ones the community votes for, and take just the parts you want.

The [shadcn themes](https://shadcnpreset.com/themes) section pulls the colour layer out of community presets as free themes. Each theme has its own page with a live preview on example components and blocks, its full palette, and the CSS or a `shadcn apply --only theme` command to use it in your project.

## Features

- **Free shadcn themes** – Themes taken from the most voted community presets, each previewed on real components in light or dark mode.
- **Copy the CSS or use the CLI** – Copy a theme's `:root` and `.dark` variables, or apply it with `npx shadcn@latest apply --preset <code> --only theme`.
- **Font pairing** – Add a heading and body font to any theme; the preview, CSS and command follow, and `--only theme,font` installs the fonts.
- **Community presets** – Browse and vote on presets shared by the community, with previews across the shadcn styles.
- **Find a preset with AI** – Describe what you're building and get matching presets shown on real components.
- **Build a page** – Arrange blocks into a page and restyle all of it by switching presets.
- **Free tools** – A preset theme CSS generator, a colour contrast checker for preset palettes, and a Figma plugin for preset variables.
